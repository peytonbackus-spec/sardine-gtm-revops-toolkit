# MEDDPICC Opportunity Inspection Framework

## Executive Summary
Standardized deal qualification methodology to audit pipeline health, eliminate false-positive opportunities, and validate stage progression.

## Inspection Checklist & Scorecard

| Pillar | Core Qualification Criteria | Required Proof / Verification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metrics** | Quantifiable economic impact (ROI, hours saved, ARR gain). | Documented business case approved by Economic Buyer. |
| **Economic Buyer** | Person with ultimate decision and budget authority. | Direct 1:1 call completed with AE. |
| **Decision Criteria** | Technical, commercial, and vendor standards. | Written RFP responses or technical sign-off doc. |
| **Decision Process** | Timeline, technical evaluation, legal, and procurement steps. | Mutually agreed Close Plan with target dates. |
| **Paper Process** | Contract review, security assessment, and vendor onboarding steps. | Redlines initiated with Legal/Infosec team. |
| **Identify Pain** | Core operational blocker requiring immediate solution. | Direct correlation between pain and buyer metrics. |
| **Champion** | Internal advocate with power, influence, and personal gain. | Champion provides insider updates on internal buying loop. |
| **Competitors** | Alternative solutions, internal builds, or status quo. | Differentiation map validated against customer needs. |

## Operational Inspection Guardrails
* **Stage 3 Gate:** Deals cannot advance past Stage 3 without a identified **Champion** and confirmed **Economic Buyer**.
* **Stage 4 Gate:** Deals cannot enter Stage 4 without an executed Mutual Action Plan (MAP) detailing the **Paper Process**.
