# Sardine Context

Full sourced brief: [sardine-company-brief.md](https://github.com/peytonbackus-spec/sardine-gtm-revops-toolkit/blob/main/shared_core/context/sardine-company-brief.md). Segments and buying committee: [icp-and-personas.md](https://github.com/peytonbackus-spec/sardine-gtm-revops-toolkit/blob/main/shared_core/context/icp-and-personas.md).

**What Sardine sells:** an agentic risk platform for fraud prevention and AML compliance on one platform. It covers device intelligence, behavioral biometrics, payment and card fraud, account takeover, KYC/KYB, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and case management, plus AI agents for alert work (KYC, sanctions, SAR generation, transaction monitoring and more).

**Who buys it:** banks and credit unions, sponsor banks, fintechs and neobanks, payments companies, crypto, marketplaces and commerce, payroll and B2B platforms, prediction markets, and federal. It has 500+ enterprise customers, and named logos include FIS, Experian, Nubank, GoDaddy, Deel, Gusto, Xero and ZoomInfo.

## The four facts that shape RevOps at Sardine

| Fact | So RevOps must… |
|---|---|
| **Consumption pricing with a minimum monthly commit** `[VERIFY]` | Track usage vs commit monthly: overage → re-commit, under-use → adoption plan. See [[Consumption and Lead to Cash]]. |
| **Two budgets: fraud and compliance** | Model them as two product lines with different economic buyers; cross-sell is the main expansion motion |
| **Bank buyers** | Add third-party-risk and model-risk review gates; treat regulatory actions as the strongest compliance trigger |
| **A global, vertical sales org** | Route by segment × region (NA, EU/UK, MENA, LATAM, APAC; crypto and federal); measure coverage and forecast bias by region |
