# VP of Sales Intake: questions for the first 1:1

Ask these before building anything recurring. Twenty minutes here saves a quarter of reports nobody reads. Record the answers in the [decision log](../../docs/overview/decision-log.md) and set the defaults in `config/company.yaml` (`sales_leadership`).

## Format and depth
1. When you open a report from me, do you want the **snapshot** (one screen: the number, three things, deals to act on, asks) or **everything** (rep, industry, stage and deal detail)? Should that change for the monthly or quarterly view?
2. Where do you read it: Slack, email, a dashboard, a doc? Phone or laptop?
3. When do you want it: Monday morning, the day before the forecast call, or on demand?
4. What is the one number you check first?

## Definitions (so we never argue about whose number is right)
5. What counts as pipeline: which stages, which forecast categories, renewals in or out?
6. Is quota on bookings, ARR, or first-year contract value? How are expansion and consumption overage counted?
7. Which win rate do you quote: count or dollars, which period?
8. Which stage do you think of as the "intro call" and which as "discovery"? Where do you feel deals slow down today?
9. When should a deal be called at risk, in your view? Anything the risk model should weigh that it doesn't?

## Reps and coaching
10. How do you want rep performance shown to you, and to managers? Ranked, or flagged only when someone needs help?
11. Who is ramping, and what is a fair expectation for each ramp quarter?
12. What should a manager do with a "Coach" flag, and by when?

## Industries and territories
13. Which segments are strategic regardless of current win rate (we keep investing even if early numbers are weak)?
14. How many closed deals before you'd trust a segment's win rate enough to move headcount?

## All-hands
15. What is the all-hands cadence and length? Who presents?
16. Recognition: which behaviours do you want to reward publicly (biggest deal, pipeline built, competitive displacement, helping a teammate)?
17. Anything that should never be shown to the whole team?

## Working together
18. What report do you get today that you'd happily stop getting?
19. What is the last question you asked that took too long to answer?
20. Who else should see the snapshot (sales managers, the CRO, Finance)?
